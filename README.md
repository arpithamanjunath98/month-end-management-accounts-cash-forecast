# Project 3 — Month-End Management Accounts, Working Capital & 13-Week Cash Forecast

## Status
**FINAL — portfolio-ready**

This project is an independent management-accounting and finance-operations case study for **Aster & Vale Consumer Products Ltd**, a fictional UK consumer-products business.

The project has two workbooks:

1. **Detailed Accounting Model V1**
   - 940-line double-entry general ledger
   - balanced opening trial balance
   - month-end accrual, prepayment, depreciation and bad-debt journals
   - adjusted trial balance
   - actual vs budget management P&L
   - balance sheet
   - bank reconciliation
   - AR and AP ageing
   - working-capital KPIs
   - 13-week cash forecast
   - close checklist and finance dashboard

2. **FINAL CFO Pack**
   - YTD variance investigation
   - balance-sheet reconciliation pack
   - working-capital action plan
   - base / downside / severe / management-action cash scenarios
   - formula-driven CFO commentary
   - CFO management pack
   - recruiter-facing portfolio summary

## Financial controls

The detailed accounting model demonstrates:
- balanced double-entry trial balance
- **£0 balance-sheet check**
- **£0 bank-reconciliation difference**
- **£0 AR subledger difference**
- AP difference limited to floating-point rounding
- doubtful-debt allowance treated as a judgemental review against modelled ECL rather than forcing a false reconciliation

## Important caveats

All data is synthetic.

Cash-release opportunities, ageing collections and scenario results are **modelled planning outputs**, not realised savings or employer results.

The V1 close checklist shows the operational close during progress. The final CFO pack presents the simulated close after review/sign-off as **100% complete**.
